# Hackathon presentation

Source files for the 10-slide deck "Political Research Monitor" (Research AI Hackathon, San Francisco,
November 2026): the research need, scope, methodology, design, tools, product UI and next steps.

The deck itself lives as a Claude Slides artifact: https://claude.ai/artifact/1nGwMGzF4LSMN4T8PmwF61
(private to its owner until shared).

- `deck.json`: slide order, sections and fonts
- `deck.css`: shared styles
- `slides/<id>.html`: one slide each, with speaker notes in `<aside>`

Slides 8 and 9 hold placeholders for three product screenshots.

In France, the media, social networks and political parties are the three least trusted of 22 organisations: 29%, 15% and 15%. The OECD sees the same pattern across its members: parties at 24%, news media at 39%, and only 31% feel they have a say. Those three channels are where the claims about politicians come from. We call them collective claims, and they are what we want to benchmark against evidence and against what people actually believe.

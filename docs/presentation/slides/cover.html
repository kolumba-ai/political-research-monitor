Political Research Monitor

Measuring the gap between what is claimed about politicians, what the evidence says and what people actually believe.

Kolumba AI

We built a research pipeline that collects what is said by and about politicians, checks it against evidence, and is designed to poll people about the same claims. This deck covers why the research is needed, its scope, the method, the design, the tools, the product and what comes next.
